KCS Capital · Independent research

We spent two years looking for the gap in Canadian financial services.
It was not settlement. It was suitability.

KCS Capital is a Canadian research firm. Between 2024 and 2026 we ran a five-sector discovery across mortgage, insurance, investing, automotive and digital assets. In every sector we found the same thing. Regulators are asking firms to prove why a recommendation fit the consumer, and most files cannot show it. So we built 4orm Finance to close that gap.

The discovery

Five sectors, one pattern.

Each sector has its own regulator, its own product, its own vocabulary. The findings converged. The consumer arrives uncertain. The firm carries the duty and cannot easily prove it discharged it. The regulator has stopped accepting signed forms as proof. Every sector below is a full KCS discovery report, published as part of the research library.

The suitability record is missing on the file that needs it most.

What we found

The standard of proof is changing. Signed forms are giving way to evidence.

Four regulatory shifts, one direction. Every Canadian sector that handles a major financial decision is being asked for something the file rarely contains: the firm's own reasoning, kept as it stood on the day the recommendation was made.

2021
Client Focused Reforms in force. Enhanced know-your-client, know-your-product and suitability requirements for investment registrants.
CSA/CIRO
2023
FSRA reviews 101 private mortgage files. Documentation gaps in about 65% of them. CIRO is formed and unifies investment oversight.
FSRA / CIRO
2025
RPAA supervision begins 8 September. CSA and CIRO review 105 investment firms. Deficiencies trigger corrective action.
Bank of Canada / CSA / CIRO
2026
Bill C-12 raises AML penalties as much as 40 times, to $20M or 3% of global revenue. BC Mortgage Services Act comes into force 13 October.
Parliament / BCFSA
What we built

4orm Finance is the operating outcome of the research.

The research pointed to one product: an evidence layer that sits beside the systems a firm already runs and turns each decision into a shared, reviewable suitability record. It never holds money. It never advises. It never replaces the workflow. It records the reasoning while the work is being done, so a review starts from evidence rather than from a search.
